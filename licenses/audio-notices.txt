behavior from audio.cpp, commit 3497b7cc44753e2c141d8fe60ac42cec433e3281.
Copyright 2026 ShugoAI LLC. Apache License 2.0; see audio-cpp-Apache-2.0.txt.
Overgo changes: Go implementation, strict RIFF/chunk and geometry validation,
bounded allocation, neutral decoded values, and artifact-owned admission.
No audio.cpp runtime or server is linked or invoked.

internal/audiodsp ports selected framing, window, filterbank, normalization and
inverse-overlap-add behavior from src/framework/audio/dsp.cpp at the same
audio.cpp commit. Changes: explicit recipe declarations instead of family
branches or defaults, strict geometry and finite-value checks, byte-budgeted
caller-owned scratch, offline chunk-boundary invariance, and refusal of
uncovered inverse samples. The Fourier numerical core consolidates Overgo's
existing projector direct-DFT reference, inherited from adaptive_new commit
214950b3b0316bcdcab38a3b95127a927c0ab5be (go/extmodel/media.go). It is not a
port of audio.cpp's bundled FFT library and does not claim its performance.

and Torch/TorchAudio 2.7.0. The selected feature extractor is copyright 2026 IBM
and The HuggingFace Team, Apache-2.0. Runtime source/library hashes, the elected

internal/speechrecognition and grouped/delta processing in internal/audiodsp
re-express the CPU encoder and processor semantics of Transformers 5.16.1
Granite Speech 5 (copyright 2026 IBM and The HuggingFace Team, Apache-2.0).
Changes: artifact-declared tensor roles and composition, existing Go host math,
