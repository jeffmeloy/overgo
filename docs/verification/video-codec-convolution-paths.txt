overgo video codec: the Wan2.1 autoencoder's convolutions by overgo's kernels and through cuDNN, and which a request takes
tool: internal/latentvideo TestLibraryCodecKeepsWithinTheKernelsMemoryAndLevels for the large clips, TestAdoptedCodecPathAgreesWithItsOwnCodeOnASmallClip and TestLibraryCodecAgreesWithItsOwnCodeOnASmallClip for the small one, at commit 685397181
model: Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, Wan2.1_VAE.pth
device: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 D, 49140 MiB, driver 616.64, CUDA toolkit 12.9
library: cuDNN 9.1.0 (cudnnGetVersion 90100), loaded for these runs from a copy already on the machine, its directory put last on the search path of the test process alone
quiet: yes. Nothing else held the device: the figures below were taken on 2026-10-10 in the minutes after the measurement run's last evaluation ended. The record this one replaces was taken beside that evaluation and said so; its seconds were four to six tenths longer on both paths, and its comparison of the paths was the same.
threshold: 4194304 values of one channel, frames by rows by columns, is the smallest clip a served autoencoder convolves through the library (internal/latentvideo libraryClipValues)

the two paths
  kernels: overgo's own (kernels/cuda/ops_f32.cu vae_causal_conv3d_f32), single precision operands and sums. Held to the host reference value for value, at 1e-5.
  library: cuDNN's forward convolution through internal/cuda/cudnn, for the stride-one convolutions, which are all but the two-to-one downsampling and the upsampling ones. Operands are handed to it in half precision and its sums kept in single, on the device's tensor units (CUDNN_CONVOLUTION_FWD_ALGO_IMPLICIT_PRECOMP_GEMM, half precision tensors, single precision compute type). A chunk is staged behind the frames before it in half precision, convolved with no padding in time, and widened to single with the bias added. The frames the next chunk reads are the last of what was staged, kept as staged: in half precision, once.
  also asked of the library and not taken: half precision sums, which it refuses for these shapes (status 3000), and single precision operands, which it convolves slower than the kernels do.

  once set up the library takes a fifth off an encode and a third off a decode: 3.19 to 2.59 and 3.12 to 2.11 seconds for 21 frames, 12.12 to 9.78 and 11.94 to 8.00 for 81. That is 0.19 seconds on each million values, frames by rows by columns, of a round trip, at both sizes, and 0.12 of a decode alone.
  setting the library up costs about a second, once in a process: the small clip's first round trip took 1.05 seconds more than its later ones. Most of that is loading it. The large clip's 40 shapes, planned after the small clip had loaded the library, made its first round trip 0.06 seconds longer than the kernels' own first run was over theirs.

values
  5 frames at 32 by 16 against the autoencoder's own code in float32 on the host (fixtures/wan/vae_clip_round_trip.json, written by scripts/python_golden_references/wan_vae_clip_round_trip.py):
    kernels  furthest value 0.000 of a level of 255, error against the source the reference's own

  the library's decoded clip against the kernels', the same latent decoded: at most 0.754 of a level over 21 frames at 832 by 480, 0.897 over 81.
  the bound is two levels and four parts in 100, the one a served reconstruction is held to (internal/mediacapability TestVideoAutoencoderRoundTripMatchesItsOwnCode).

what a request takes
  a served autoencoder convolves a clip of at least the threshold above through the library, on a machine that has it, and any smaller clip by the kernels. 21 frames at 832 by 480 are 8,386,560 values and take it. A picture at 832 by 480 is 399,360 and does not, and a process that serves only pictures never loads the library.
  written down before the run: a path is adopted only when every bound holds and the whole request is faster over three repetitions of each clip, with setup counted. By that rule the library is taken for the clips of 21 and of 81 frames, 14.60 seconds against 19.40 and 53.95 against 72.78, and not for the clip of 5 frames at 32 by 16, 1.40 against 0.63.
  at the threshold a round trip saves 0.81 seconds, so the setup's second is repaid in two requests, and over the rule's three repetitions the library is ahead by 1.4 seconds; a clip of twice the threshold, which the 21 frames are, repays it within its own request. A picture saves 0.08 seconds a round trip and would need about fourteen to repay it.
  the codec's own code encodes the 21 frames in 0.7 to 1.0 seconds and decodes them in 1.25 on this device. Neither path reaches that. What is left outside the library is the two-to-one downsampling convolution and the upsampling one, the normalisation, the attention and the copying of temporal caches.

what a user installs
  nothing is required. A machine without cuDNN serves every request by the kernels, as before, and setup-steps holds.
  for the library: cuDNN 9 for CUDA 12, from NVIDIA under NVIDIA's own license, with the directory that holds cudnn64_9.dll on the search path, or its files in the bin directory CUDA_PATH names. The convolution loads seven of its files, 797,228,328 bytes: cudnn64_9.dll, cudnn_graph64_9.dll, cudnn_ops64_9.dll, cudnn_cnn64_9.dll, cudnn_engines_precompiled64_9.dll, cudnn_heuristic64_9.dll, cudnn_engines_runtime_compiled64_9.dll. overgo ships none of them.
  a clip convolved through the library is not, value for value, the clip the kernels make, so the two are kept under different execution identities (internal/inferencesurface names the seven files), and a frozen clip accepted under one is not the other's.
