What was asked. An open of the store loads its newest checkpoint generation and replays the journal
after it, and every model load opens the store. The plan's row process-records-out asked what that
generation holds by record family, in bytes and in decode time, which code writes each family, and
which families a model load, an evaluation, a training run and the scorecard read. A family none of
them reads is the development process's by the row's rule, and the step that follows is filed from
this record. Nothing was moved or deleted for it: the command opens the store to read it and reads
the generation's files, and writes nothing.

What a family is. A family is the key that lets a reader name the code that wrote a record.

  artifacts  The descriptor's schema and the leading part of the key of the commit that introduced
             it, written "schema by key". One schema has several writers, a run record an evaluation
             writes and one the old gate wrote, and the key tells them apart. A descriptor that
             states no schema is written "no schema: kind media-type by key", with "untyped" where it
             states no media type and "bare" where it is an identity a batch declared and did not
             carry.
  contents, locations  The family of the artifact the record belongs to.
  lineage    The family of the edge's child, the document that declares the edge.
  commits    The leading part of the batch key.
  aliases    The leading part of the name.
  causality  The link's trigger. This generation holds no causal link.

The leading part of a key or a name is its text before the second of the separators it is built
with, a slash, a colon or a full stop, and before the first when what follows it is empty or holds
a digit: gate/package/evidence:sha256:05a0 is gate/package and rebuild/000003 is rebuild. A store
rebuild commits another store's records again under its own key (internal/overgodb/rebuild.go:88),
so "by rebuild" names the rebuild and not the code that first wrote the record; the schema names
that.

How the bytes are attributed. A member of the generation is a file: a header line and a body, and
the body is a compact JSON array of records. A record owns its own bytes and the separator after
it, so the attribution is exact: a projection's bytes are its header, its framing (the array's
opening bracket, or both brackets of an empty array) and the bytes of its families. A projection's
families that each hold less than 0.05% of the generation are counted together in its line "rest",
which says how many they are. A family's lines are

  family <projection> <bytes> <records> <share of the generation> <last> <name>

where last is the latest commit sequence among the family's records: a commit's own, and for every
other record the sequence that introduced the artifact it belongs to, an alias's target and an
edge's child among them. A family whose last sequence is far behind the generation's is one that
nothing writes any more.

How the times were taken. Every time is in microseconds, read with the repository's counter
(internal/processmeasure), and is the middle of nine readings beside the least and the most; each
reading follows a collection, so it starts as an open does, on a heap that holds little. A
projection's decode time is the restore of its body alone into an empty facet, which is what an
open does with a member once it has read and verified it. The line "set" is the load of the whole
generation as an open makes it: all seven members read, verified and restored at once. An open
restores the members at once, so the set's load is about its slowest member's restore and far less
than the sum of the seven.

How the table was made. The table that follows the census was written by reading the tree. A
writer is the place a record of the family is committed, or the place its key or alias is built;
"none in the tree" says nothing at this commit builds the family's schema or key. The four readers
are a model load for serving (internal/modelcli, internal/inference, internal/modelrecipe,
internal/discovery), an evaluation (cmd/evaluate, internal/evaluation), a training run (cmd/train,
internal/trainingworkflow) and the scorecard (internal/scorecard, cmd/validate -scorecard), each
with what it calls. A reader is a place where one of them decodes a document of the family's
schema or resolves a name of the family; "none found" says a search of those packages for the
schema, for the functions of its codec and for the key or alias prefix found none. A walk of the
whole catalog that decodes no document of the family is not counted. A family is classed "read"
when a reader was found and "development process" when none was. The class is the row's rule and
no more: it does not say a family is worth nothing, and two of these families are read by a test.
The table has an entry for every family whose printed lines hold 1% of the generation or more, and
for the smaller families of the same deleted writers. An entry's bytes are those of its printed
lines; where a family is under the floor in one projection, that part is in the projection's rest.
