library: cuDNN 9.1.0 (cudnnGetVersion 90100), loaded from the directory of libraries beside overgo's data (internal/dataroot Roots.Libraries), where a copy of it was put on 2026-10-10
quiet: yes. The test that times the large clips takes the device alone (internal/cuda/testutil MeasurementProcess): it waits for the device's other users to finish and they wait for it. The figures below were taken so on 2026-10-10, between the evaluations of a measurement run, and the small clip's by a build of its tests that takes the device the same way. The kernels' seconds stand within 0.07 of the record this one replaces, which was taken on a device nothing else used.
threshold: 4194304 values of one channel, frames by rows by columns, is the smallest clip a served autoencoder convolves through the library (internal/latentvideo libraryClipValues)

the two paths
  kernels: overgo's own (kernels/cuda/ops_f32.cu vae_causal_conv3d_f32, vae_downsample2d_f32, vae_upsample2d_f32, vae_temporal_downsample_f32), single precision operands and sums, double precision sums where a frame or a pair of frames is convolved to half. Held to the host reference value for value, at 1e-5.
  library: cuDNN's forward convolution through internal/cuda/cudnn, for every convolution of a clip. Operands are handed to it in half precision and its sums kept in single, on the device's tensor units (CUDNN_CONVOLUTION_FWD_ALGO_IMPLICIT_PRECOMP_GEMM, half precision tensors, single precision compute type). Its input is staged in half precision by a kernel, convolved, and widened to single with the bias added. A convolution at stride one has its chunk staged behind the frames before it and is convolved with no padding in time; the frames the next chunk reads are the last of what was staged, kept as staged, in half precision, once. A frame convolved to half its extents is staged with the row of zeros below it and the column to its right that the kernel reads past its edge, and convolved at stride two. A frame enlarged to twice its extents is staged enlarged, each value at four positions, and convolved at stride one. Two frames convolved to one are staged behind the frame before them and convolved over three frames at stride two. A frame of an odd extent and a chunk of an odd count of frames are left to the kernels; no served size has either.
  also asked of the library and not taken: half precision sums, which it refuses for these shapes (status 3000), and single precision operands, which it convolves slower than the kernels do.

what a request takes
  a served autoencoder convolves a clip of at least the threshold above through the library, on a machine that has it, and any smaller clip by the kernels. 21 frames at 832 by 480 are 8,386,560 values and take it. A picture at 832 by 480 is 399,360 and does not, and a process that serves only pictures never loads the library.
  written down before the first run: a path is adopted only when every bound holds and the whole request is faster over three repetitions of each clip, with setup counted. By that rule the library is taken for the clips of 21 and of 81 frames, 10.96 seconds against 19.79 and 39.13 against 73.50.
  the threshold was set when the library saved 0.19 seconds on each million values of a round trip and cost a second to set up, so that a clip at the threshold repaid it in two requests. It saves 0.36 now, and a clip at the threshold saves 1.5 seconds a round trip and repays the first use in its own request.
  not decided here: whether a smaller clip or a picture should take the library too. The clip of 5 frames at 32 by 16 now passes the rule above, 0.40 seconds against 0.65 over three round trips with the library's first use in them, where it failed it before this record, 1.40 against 0.63: the kinds that were left on the kernels were most of a small clip's time. A served autoencoder still keeps such a clip on the kernels, by the threshold, which this change did not move. A picture was not timed, a first use from a cold disk was not timed, and the accepted pictures and small clips are the kernels' bytes on every machine, which moving the threshold would end. That is the row codec-library-threshold.
  the codec's own code encodes the 21 frames in 0.7 to 1.0 seconds and decodes them in 1.25 on this device; the library's path takes 1.38 and 2.03. What is left outside the library is the normalisation, the attention and the copying of temporal caches.

what a user installs
  nothing is required. A machine without cuDNN serves every request by the kernels, as before, and setup-steps holds.
  for the library: cuDNN 9 for CUDA 12, from NVIDIA under NVIDIA's own license, with the directory that holds cudnn64_9.dll on the search path, or its files in the bin directory CUDA_PATH names, or in the directory of libraries beside overgo's data. The convolution loads seven of its files, 797,228,328 bytes: cudnn64_9.dll, cudnn_graph64_9.dll, cudnn_ops64_9.dll, cudnn_cnn64_9.dll, cudnn_engines_precompiled64_9.dll, cudnn_heuristic64_9.dll, cudnn_engines_runtime_compiled64_9.dll. overgo ships none of them.
  a clip convolved through the library is not, value for value, the clip the kernels make, so the two are kept under different execution identities (internal/inferencesurface names the seven files), and a frozen clip accepted under one is not the other's.
