model: Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B (30-block conditioned diffusion transformer, 1,418,996,800 F32 parameters)

This run trains the COMPLETE diffusion transformer — every block, the patch
embedding, the text and time embeddings, the time projection, and the
modulated head — with the frozen text encoder and VAE outside the trainable
surface. The objective is the pipeline's own flow-matching contract: MSE
against v = noise - x0 on the shifted flow schedule (shift 5, the committed
g2/g3 schedule).

Text conditioning: the raw umt5-xxl encoder rows for the committed g1 fox
prompt ("a red fox licking a vanilla ice cream cone on a snowy pine forest
trail"), computed by the repository's streamed host encoder from the real
models_t5_umt5-xxl-enc-bf16.pth checkpoint — the exact pre-projection rows
the trainable text embedding consumes. The trainer's own projection of these
rows is logged against the committed raw/g1_cond_context.f32le capture.

Timestep conditioning: computed from the trainable time embedding/projection
tensors at a committed g2 schedule timestep; the trainer's conditioning path
is bit-parity gated against raw/g2_block_e.f32le / raw/g2_head_e.f32le.

x0: the committed g3 final latent (fixtures/wan/raw/g3_final_latent.f32le,
sha256-pinned — the real denoised fox latent [16,1,2,4]) tiled spatially to
the training grid; noise from the golden Philox plan (seed 31); x_t is the
real flow mixture (1-sigma)*x0 + sigma*noise. No demo video ships under the
model directory, so the committed real latent is the x0 seed grid — declared
plainly rather than synthesizing from statistics.

The fixtures live in this repository (fixtures/wan) and are sha256-verified
at load; this declaration grounds the training-recipe dataset authority by
content.
