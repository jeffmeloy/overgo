block, the widened 32-channel patch embedding, the checkpoint's own text

stops at them (the source is data, exactly as in serving).

run (the committed g1 fox prompt through the streamed host encoder),
projected by the LiveEdit checkpoint's own trainable text_embedding tensors.

(fixtures/wan/raw/g4_final_latent.f32le, sha256-pinned real denoised latent)

Philox plan (seed 31). No demo video ships under the model directory, so the
committed real latents are the seed grids — declared plainly rather than
