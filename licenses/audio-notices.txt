finite-memory frame classifier and offline/live boundary policies from
FireRedVAD c30ec49e8cc69642b0ee65362eba11b9d11c6e54. Copyright 2026 Xiaohongshu;

is shipped or invoked by Go execution. Numerical comparison is not an annotated
